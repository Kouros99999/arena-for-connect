Support

Email support@arenaforconnect.com. We answer within one business day.

What to include

  • The AWS region and the name of your Arena CloudFormation stack.
  • What you expected and what happened, with the time it happened.
  • For sign-in or scoring questions, the agent's Connect username. Please do not send customer contact content.

Documentation

Setup guides and how each feature works are in the help center. Admin scripts and the issue tracker are on GitHub.

Billing and cancellation

Subscriptions are billed by AWS Marketplace per active agent-day. Cancel at any time from your AWS Marketplace subscriptions page. Deleting the CloudFormation stack removes Arena and all of its data from your account.
