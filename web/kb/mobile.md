# Arena on your phone

The supervisor console, results report and agent panel work on a phone, and they install as an app so they open full screen from the home screen with the stack's own icon. Nothing to download from a store, and the same sign-in as on a desktop.

## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the console's address in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the share button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Arena from the home screen. It runs full screen, with the ◐ button for light or dark and the same Brand, People and Data buttons as on a desktop.

## Install on Android

1. Open the console's address in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## What works offline

The pages themselves open without a connection, so the app never shows a blank screen. Live numbers need the network: when the API cannot be reached the page says so and keeps the last figures it showed until the connection returns.

## For supervisors on the floor

The console on a phone keeps the stats, flags, challenges, reward approvals, kudos feed and coaching plans in one column. The leaderboard scrolls sideways. Approving a reward, ending a challenge, starting a suggested one or saving a brand works exactly as on a desktop.

## Updates

The app picks up a new version the next time it is opened with a connection; no reinstall is needed.
