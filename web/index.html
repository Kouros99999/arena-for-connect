How it is delivered

Arena is sold through AWS Marketplace and deploys as one CloudFormation stack into your AWS account. Agent data never leaves that account. Setup is a launch link, two settings in the Connect console, and users synced from your Connect directory.

The demo pages above run on simulated data in your browser.
