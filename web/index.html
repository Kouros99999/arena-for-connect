Questions people ask

Does it work with our existing agent workspace?

Arena is a third-party application in the Amazon Connect agent workspace, which means it opens as a tab beside the contact. It needs the standard workspace; custom contact control panels can open the panel in a browser tab instead.

Can agents game it?

Scores come only from Connect data. Kudos are capped per day, cannot be sent to yourself, and unusual kudos volume is flagged. Races have minimum qualifiers so a one-call wonder cannot win on a single evaluation.

What do agents see of each other?

Their own team's leaderboard and feed. Coaching plans are visible only to the agent concerned and their supervisors, and the supervisor's private note stays private.

Can we use our own identity provider?

Yes. Federate it to the user pool the stack creates, or launch with your provider's issuer and audience. The sign-in guide explains both.

How do we get our data out?

It is in a DynamoDB table in your own account. Export it to S3 for your warehouse, or delete the stack and it is gone. Per-agent deletion is built in.
