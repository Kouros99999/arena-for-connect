How the arithmetic works

  • Attrition savings = agents × attrition rate × attrition reduction × cost to replace one agent. With the defaults: 100 × 35% × 15% × $12,000.
  • Productivity = agents × fully loaded cost × productivity gain. A 3% gain across 100 agents is three agents' worth of capacity you do not have to hire. If you would not redeploy it, set it to zero.
  • Arena subscription = agents × working days × 11 months × $0.75, since the first 30 days are free for up to 25 agents a day. Agents with no contacts on a day are not billed, so this is the ceiling.
  • Stack cost is the AWS resources Arena runs on in your account, about $15 a month for a 500-agent centre. $180 a year is used here.
  • Not counted: supervisor time saved by flags and suggested challenges, the effect of coaching with before-and-after results on evaluation scores, customer sentiment, or anything you cannot put a number on. The calculator is meant to be beaten, not believed.

Where the defaults come from: replacing a contact centre agent is widely estimated at $10,000 to $20,000 once recruiting, training and the months to full productivity are counted, and annual attrition of 30 to 45 percent is typical. The 15 percent attrition reduction and 3 percent productivity gain are at the low end of what published recognition and gamification programmes report. Your own numbers are better than anyone's averages.
